Who we are

DANLstudio publishes Idle Delivery Man, a mobile delivery-management game. This policy explains the information handled by the game and its advertising, purchase and platform providers. Contact us at danliosteam2025@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, in-game name, currency balances, courier collection, goals, settings and records used to prevent duplicate rewards are saved locally. We do not provide a game-account registration system or a public player chat. The in-game name is not sent to our advertising or purchase providers as a customer attribute.

Optional game reminders are local device notifications. The game does not register a remote push-notification token for these reminders. Your operating system may include app data in its own backups according to your device settings.

Advertising and consent

The game includes optional rewarded advertising provided by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy messages and choices. Test builds may use Google's test advertisements.

Google's advertising SDK may collect or share the device IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or app-set identifiers, ad and app interactions, and technical diagnostics. These are used for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. The information processed can depend on your region, consent choices, device settings and ad configuration.

Where required, an applicable consent message is requested before advertising. When Google makes privacy options available, they can be opened from the game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android settings. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy controls.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Optional in-app purchases are processed by the platform store, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store. The game uses RevenueCat to validate transactions, provide purchased content, restore eligible purchases and prevent duplicate grants.

RevenueCat processes an app-specific anonymous customer identifier, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase and entitlement status, and technical information needed for the service, such as app and SDK versions, platform, locale and currency. We do not attach your in-game name, email address or telephone number to the RevenueCat profile. We have disabled RevenueCat's optional diagnostics in our integration.

The store handles payment credentials. We do not receive your full payment-card number, payment password or store-account password. Purchase records can remain with the store and RevenueCat after the app is removed. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and the privacy information for your platform store.

Support and platform services

If you email support, we process the email address, message and any screenshots, order references or diagnostic details you choose to send to resolve your request. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

The game uses the Unity runtime. Operating systems, stores and testing services may separately process device, purchase, crash or beta-feedback information under their own policies. We do not operate a separate gameplay-analytics backend. Relevant provider information includes Unity's player privacy information and Apple's privacy information.

Why information is used

Information is used to run the game, deliver and restore purchases, show optional ads, respect privacy choices, prevent fraud, maintain security and answer support requests. Where applicable, the basis is providing requested services, consent for advertising or related identifiers when required, legitimate interests in support and security, and legal record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your saved game files or provide them to data brokers. Advertising providers may use or share advertising-related information as described above and in their policies; your regional privacy choices apply.

Storage, transfers and security

Google AdMob and RevenueCat transmit their service data over encrypted connections. Their systems may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

Local game data remains until you clear the app data, remove the app or restore a device backup. Support correspondence and provider-side records are kept as needed to provide support, maintain purchase entitlements, meet legal obligations and prevent fraud. RevenueCat customer records may remain until deletion is requested or while needed for those purposes. Advertising-provider retention is governed by Google's policy. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

Request data deletion

Email danliosteam2025@gmail.com with the subject “Idle Delivery Man data deletion”. Tell us the platform you use and, if relevant, an approximate purchase date or store order reference so we can locate the correct purchase-service record. We may need additional information to verify the request.

You can remove local progress by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game. This does not automatically erase store or RevenueCat records. We can handle requests for support correspondence and identifiable RevenueCat customer records under our control. Some transaction, security or legally required records may need to be retained. Google's account and advertising data is managed through Google's privacy controls. Deleting game data does not refund a purchase or remove a store transaction.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact the email above to exercise rights concerning information under our control. For information held independently by a platform or provider, use that provider's controls or contact process.

Age and children

The current version is intended for players aged 16 and older and is not directed to younger children. Store-assigned age ratings and local rules also apply. It includes optional purchases of virtual currency and randomized in-game rewards. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our support or services, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when features, providers or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Когда Google предоставляет параметры конфиденциальности, они доступны из настроек игры; рекламный идентификатор также управляется в настройках Android. Подробности — в политике Google.

Покупки

Необязательные покупки обрабатывает магазин платформы. RevenueCat проверяет транзакции, права на покупки и восстановление, помогает исключать повторные начисления. Обрабатываются анонимный идентификатор клиента приложения, история покупок, идентификаторы товаров и транзакций, статусы покупок и необходимые технические сведения: платформа, версии приложения и SDK, локаль и валюта. Игровое имя, email и телефон мы к профилю RevenueCat не прикрепляем; дополнительная диагностика RevenueCat отключена. Полные данные карты и пароли магазина нам не поступают. История магазина и RevenueCat может сохраняться после удаления приложения. См. политику RevenueCat.
